Timeline: gap, considered, verdict, as built

One row per part of the change, in its system's colour. Read a row left to right: the gap the research found at the baseline, every option the design weighed (the filled mark is the one taken), the verdict and its defence, and what was built. A dashed fourth cell is not built yet. Click a cell or an option for its detail.

Decision log

Every option from every row, with its verdict and the reason. Declined options stay on record: they are what a later plan must argue against before reopening a question.

As built: what exists, and every change from the verdict

One card per part. Its state says how the built thing relates to the verdict; each change names what moved, why, and in which stage or review it was decided. The verdict column is never edited to match: this register is where the difference lives.

plan.json the committed file, embedded verbatim

The page is built from this file at load. The planner writes it; the implementer and the presenter update the fourth column and the status; scripts/embed.js copies it in here and scripts/check.js verifies the copy and the rules.
